Pocket Electronic: A font for you pencil case!

Pocket Electronic is a variable font inspired by the classic LED displays our calculator-loving selves knew and loved. Sharp numerals, round dots — it merges the precision of early digital type with the warmth of dot-matrix grids.

Two axes let you push it from crisp and technical to soft and rounded. Whether you're building something that needs a retro-tech edge or just needs a bit of personality, Pocket Electronic fits right in your pencil case.

Two axes. Endless moods.

The DOT axis shifts the circular terminals between perfect spheres and crisp squares. The BAR axis works on the stroke connectors — rounding the joints or sharpening them back to corners.

Mix and match: soft dots with sharp bars feel relaxed but precise. Sharp dots with round bars feel retrofuturist. Every combination has a different character.
